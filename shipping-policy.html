MB1 Shipping Policy

We use size-based standard shipping so customers know what to expect at checkout. Larger signs require a destination-specific shipping quote because carrier oversize charges can vary substantially.

Free standard shipping applies to qualifying shipments within the contiguous United States. Alaska, Hawaii, remote locations, special handling, and other nonstandard destinations may require an additional quote.

36" and 48" Oversized Signs

Shipping is quoted separately before payment.

These packages can trigger carrier dimensional-weight, additional-handling, or large-package charges. MB1 will determine the practical shipping method after the destination is known and provide the final shipping charge and Stripe payment instructions before the order is completed.

This protects the customer from an inflated one-size-fits-all freight charge and protects MB1 from absorbing unusually high carrier surcharges.

Carrier Selection

MB1 will compare practical USPS and UPS options for standard parcels and use the most appropriate service for the package size and destination. Oversized orders may require a specialized UPS or freight solution.
